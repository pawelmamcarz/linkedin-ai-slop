Sensitivity-only changes recalculate cached assessments locally, including the first settings save with empty storage. Disabled evaluation stays disabled during these changes. Enabling after disabled startup registers observers. Queued cards and cards scrolled away during asynchronous expansion are checked before sending.

Post normalization preserves paragraphs, strips nested reshared content even in detached fallback clones, and keys cache entries by rubric version and formatted text. The revised Jev questions assess observable writing and meaningful detail rather than actual authorship or the mere presence of numbers.
