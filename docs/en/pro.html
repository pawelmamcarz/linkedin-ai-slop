Plans

LinkedIn only. The extension is free. The paid plans are Hosted Pro, Lifetime Pro, and Team. Checkout is on. The threshold slider (about 0.15 to 0.95, default 0.65) lives in the extension options. Lower means more „AI slop” badges. How the slider works.

Free / BYOK

You run server/ and put your own TYPESAFE_API_KEY in server/.env. In the extension options pick BYOK and the proxy URL. The key never enters the extension. This mode is live.

Hosted Demo

The default URL https://proxy-production-ebcc.up.railway.app is a public demo, not a paid plan. The per-minute cap is about 60 scores per IP. The daily brake is in the code: DEMO_MODE=1 means 200 scores per day when EVALUATE_DAILY_IP_CAP is unset. On Railway that brake applies only after the variable is set. Leave DEMO_MODE unset for local BYOK.

Hosted Pro

Price: from about 5 USD per month or about 40 USD per year. Monthly or yearly subscription via Stripe Checkout.

The public proxy with higher limits. After you pay, you get a Pro token and paste it in the extension options. Demo has lower daily caps. Pro is for heavier use.

Checkout: about 5 USD / month Checkout: about 40 USD / year

Lifetime Pro

Price: 199 PLN, one time. Launch offer. The sales cap is LIFETIME_PRO_CAP (default 50). After payment you get one Pro token that does not expire. Cancelling Hosted Pro or Team does not revoke it.

Checkout: Lifetime Pro, 199 PLN

Team

Price: 990 PLN per year for 10 seats. Yearly Stripe Checkout subscription. After payment you get 10 Pro tokens, one per install. Paste each token the same way as Hosted Pro. Cancelling the subscription revokes all 10. Checkout collects a tax ID and billing address for the invoice.

Checkout: Team, 990 PLN / year
