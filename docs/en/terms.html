Terms of use

Short terms for the free extension and the public demo proxy. Code: github.com/pawelmamcarz/linkedin-ai-slop.

What you get

LinkedIn AI Slop marks posts on the LinkedIn feed with Human, Mixed, or AI slop (the on-screen labels are Ludzki, Mieszany, AI slop). The public proxy (https://proxy-production-ebcc.up.railway.app) is a free demo. It is best-effort, with no SLA and no availability warranty. The product is for LinkedIn only.

Limits

The demo accepts up to 60 POST /evaluate requests per minute from one IP address. Text is cut at 6000 characters. Repeated, normalized text may get a cached verdict and then does not call the model again. The operator may turn on a daily cap with EVALUATE_DAILY_IP_CAP or DEMO_MODE. Hosted Pro (a token, higher limits, about 5 USD per month or 40 USD per year) works when the operator turns Stripe on. Otherwise run your own proxy and your own key.

No warranty

A badge is a model signal, not a fact and not advice. There is no warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose. The author is not liable for decisions made from a badge, or for downtime of the demo.

No abuse

Do not use the demo to scrape LinkedIn, evade limits, resell scores, or send traffic that does not come from normal feed scrolling. Do not try to extract the API key. Abuse can end in an IP block or in the public proxy being turned off.
